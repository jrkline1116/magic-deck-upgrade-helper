Get a key at console.anthropic.com/settings/keys. It stays in this browser and is sent only to Anthropic. A typical scan costs a few cents.

Google Sheets buy list

Sign in with Google and the app makes a buy list spreadsheet in your Google Drive, with a tab for each deck. Save any upgrade to it along with the card it would replace. Your decks also follow you to any device where you sign in. The app can only see the files it creates, nothing else in your Drive.

AI review

AI review is optional. It runs on your own Anthropic API key, which stays in this browser and is sent only to Anthropic. A typical scan costs a few cents.

Get a key at console.anthropic.com/settings/keys.

Stored data

Decks and card role tags are saved in this browser only. Clearing tags makes the next scan re-check each card's role.
