Privacy Policy

Upgrade Scraper. Last updated September 29, 2026.

Upgrade Scraper is a free web app that suggests upgrades for Magic: The Gathering Commander decks. It has no servers that store your data. Everything described below happens in your own browser or in your own Google account.

What stays in your browser

Your decklists, settings and, if you add one, your Anthropic API key are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. They are not sent to the app's developer. You can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Google sign-in (optional)

If you choose "Connect Google Sheets," the app asks Google for three permissions:

drive.file lets the app create one spreadsheet in your Google Drive and read and edit only that spreadsheet. The app cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.
openid and email let the app show which Google account is connected.

The app uses this access only to create your buy list spreadsheet, add a tab for each deck, save the cards you choose, and keep your decklists in sync between your devices through a hidden tab in that spreadsheet. Your Google data goes directly between your browser and Google. It is not sent to, stored by, or shared with the app's developer or anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.

You can disconnect in the app's Settings, and you can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. The spreadsheet stays in your Drive and belongs to you.

Upgrade Scraper's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services the app talks to

Scryfall (card data, images and prices): card names from your deck are sent to look them up.
Archidekt, Moxfield and MTGGoldfish: only when you paste a deck link, to read that deck. Requests may pass through a small relay that forwards them and keeps nothing.
Anthropic: only if you turn on AI review with your own API key. Your deck and candidate cards are sent to Anthropic to get suggestions.
